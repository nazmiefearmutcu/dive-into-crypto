DVCRY Deep Crypto Market Monitoring Tool

Monitor Binance USDT-M perpetual futures with technical indicators, multi-timeframe analysis and market-positioning context.

DVCRY brings together:
- 60 technical indicators across 12 timeframes.
- A ranked scanner and detailed symbol analysis.
- Indicator consensus, confidence scores and risk context.
- Open interest, funding and long/short positioning data.
- A local portfolio view for positions you enter manually.

The app uses public market data and does not require exchange credentials. It does not place orders, hold funds or provide a cryptocurrency wallet.

Scores describe indicator agreement, not the probability of a profitable trade. Market data may be delayed, incomplete or unavailable because of provider restrictions, rate limits or connection problems.

DVCRY is a market research and analysis tool. It does not provide personalized investment advice or guarantee accuracy or profitability.
